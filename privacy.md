      <h1>Privacy Policy for RunMetric</h1>
      <p class="subtitle">Last Updated: September 24, 2026</p>

      <p>RunMetric ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the RunMetric mobile application (the "App").</p>

      <p>By downloading and using RunMetric, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.</p>

      <h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>
      <p>RunMetric is designed with a privacy-first approach. We collect only the minimum amount of data required to provide you with core running metric calculations and app functionality.</p>

      <h3>A. Information Provided Directly by You</h3>
      <p><strong>Workout & Running Data:</strong> Data you manually input or track within the app, such as distance, run duration, split times, target paces, and workout history.</p>
      <p><strong>App Preferences:</strong> Settings you customize within the app, such as preferred units of measurement (kilometers vs. miles).</p>

      <h3>B. Automatically Collected Information</h3>
      <p><strong>Device and Usage Data:</strong> Basic technical data such as your device model, operating system version, app version, and basic performance or crash diagnostic logs to help us keep the app stable and bug-free.</p>

      <h3>C. Information We Do NOT Collect</h3>
      <p><strong>No Account Creation:</strong> RunMetric does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal identifiers like your full name, email address, or phone number.</p>
      <p><strong>No Exact Background Location Tracking:</strong> Unless explicitly requested for core GPS running routes in future features, core calculations (pace, split times, targets) do not harvest or store your background location history on external servers.</p>

      <h2>2. How We Use Your Information</h2>
      <p>We use the data collected strictly for the following purposes:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>To Provide Core Features:</strong> Calculating running metrics, split breakdowns, target paces, and displaying your workout history.</li>
        <li><strong>To Improve App Performance:</strong> Analyzing anonymized crash reports and app performance data to fix bugs and enhance user experience.</li>
        <li><strong>To Store Settings Locally:</strong> Saving your custom units (metric/imperial) and local workout logs on your device.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>3. Data Storage and Retention</h2>
      <p><strong>Local Storage First:</strong> Your running logs, pace calculations, and user settings are stored locally on your mobile device.</p>
      <p><strong>Data Control:</strong> Because your data is saved locally, deleting the app or clearing app data will permanently remove your stored running logs. You retain full control over your workout data at all times.</p>

      <h2>4. Third-Party Services and Analytics</h2>
      <p>We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties.</p>
      <p>Depending on the tools integrated into the App (such as Google Play Services or crash reporting frameworks), third-party services may collect basic device diagnostics:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Google Play Services:</strong> Used for app distribution, updates, and standard device performance analytics. Refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>5. Children's Privacy</h2>
      <p>RunMetric does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions to remove it.</p>

      <h2>6. Security</h2>
      <p>We value your trust in using RunMetric. Since data is maintained locally on your device, its security largely relies on your device’s security settings (such as passcodes or biometric lock). We recommend keeping your mobile device operating system updated to ensure optimal security.</p>
